AbML describes a molecule as chains of domains. Chains are separated by |, domains within a chain by -, reading N-terminus to C-terminus.

Qualifiers

.a – .hspecificity, picks the colour
(3)domain identifier
(3:10)identifier, paired with domain 10
{2}disulfides across that pairing
[ANTI:CD3]comment — also MOD, TYPE, LENGTH, CLASS, NOTE, ASEQ, DSEQ
|[ADC]trailing pseudo-chain: nonspecific conjugation

A shared light chain carries both specificities: heavy chains VH.a and VH.b against a common VL.ab. Knob and hole cannot sit on one domain, nor can both charges, and ! is only valid on CH2.

Worked example

VH.a(1:6)-CH1(2:7){1}-H(3:10){2}-CH2(4:11)-CH3>(5:12)

One knob-bearing heavy chain: VH of specificity a paired with domain 6, CH1 paired with 7 through one disulfide, hinge paired with the other hinge through two, then CH2 and a knobbed CH3.
